Public Listing Availability

When enabled, Sale Pending inventory remains visible in public inventory/search and on its public detail page. Pending Review listings are always private.

When enabled, sold inventory remains visible publicly until it is removed manually or reaches the configured sold-retention limit.

Inventory Backup / Restore

Seller export is always available for their own inventory. Seller restore is disabled by default because a trusted AutoGrid360 bundle can preserve Active, Sale Pending, Sold, Expired, Removed, and Pending Review states. Enable this only when ordinary sellers are trusted to restore their own inventory. Administrators and operators can always use the dedicated backup/restore workflow.
