Inventory Backup / Restore

AutoGrid360 inventory bundles are portable ZIP backups containing listing and vehicle data, lifecycle state/history, stable automotive reference keys, seller-profile metadata, and listing images. Passwords, sessions, roles, MFA data, and account email are never exported.

Export Inventory

Export all of your AutoGrid360 listings exactly as they currently exist, including Draft, Pending Review, Active, Sale Pending, Sold, Expired, and Removed inventory. Images and lifecycle timestamps are included. Export is always available for your own inventory.
